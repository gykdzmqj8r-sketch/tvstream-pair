Sofá TV

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Sofá TV is a media player for Samsung Smart TVs. It does not sell or provide any content, and it does not show ads or use analytics or tracking. This policy explains the little data the app and its companion web pages handle, and why.

1. Data that stays on your TV

The following is stored only in the app's storage on your TV and is never sent to us:

  • The playlists you add (addresses, usernames and passwords).
  • Your favorites, viewing history, hidden items and settings.
  • The local network addresses you add to browse shared files.

Video plays directly from the sources you choose (your playlists, USB drives, your local network or free-to-air TV). We do not receive or see what you watch.

2. Data we receive

Free trial and subscription check. To grant the free trial and check whether this TV has an active Sofá TV Pro subscription, the app sends our server the TV's device identifier (DUID, or the network MAC address if the DUID is not available), the TV model and the app version. The identifier is stored only as an irreversible SHA-256 hash, together with the trial dates and the date of the last check. A short code derived from it is shown on the subscription screen so that you and our support can identify your TV.

Subscription purchase. Sofá TV Pro is purchased on your phone, on a payment page operated by Stripe. Your payment details are handled by Stripe and are never shared with us. We receive from Stripe your email address, the plan, the subscription status and expiry date, and the Stripe subscription identifier, and link them to your TV's code.

Support chat. If you contact us through the support chat on the subscription page, we store your messages and our replies, linked to your TV's code.

Setting up a playlist with your phone. If you use the QR code to send a playlist from your phone to the TV, the details you type on the phone pass through our pairing service only to reach your TV. They are deleted as soon as the TV receives them, and the pairing code expires after 15 minutes.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the free trial and the subscription, to answer support requests and to prevent abuse of the free trial. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase, which hosts our server and database.
  • Stripe, which processes subscription payments.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the phone pages opened from the QR codes.

5. How long we keep it

We keep device and subscription records while the app is in use and for as long as needed to provide the service, meet accounting and legal obligations and resolve disputes. Support messages are kept while needed to answer you. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, including under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Email us with the code shown on the subscription screen of your TV so we can find your records. An active subscription must be canceled first. We may keep the hashed device identifier, which does not identify you, only to stop the free trial from being restarted.

7. Children

Sofá TV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
